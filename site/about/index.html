About Upgrade

A Bahrain-based brokerage business built around clear commercial introductions.

Upgrade operates as a Commodity Contracts Brokerage company. Its role is to help commercial parties understand a requirement, identify relevant potential counterparties and coordinate the discussions that may follow.

Business starts before the contract.

Before two parties can discuss terms, someone has to understand the requirement clearly enough to know which conversation is worth opening. That is where Upgrade works.

We work as the broker: clarifying the requirement, considering relevant commercial relationships and coordinating the introduction.
