Commodity Contracts Brokerage

The work sits between a commercial requirement and a meaningful introduction.

Upgrade helps the parties reach the conversation with better clarity. The transaction itself remains between the parties.

From Requirement to Conversation
01

Understand the requirement

The conversation begins with what is actually needed: product, specification, quantity, destination, timeframe and available documentation.

02

Consider relevant counterparties

Once the requirement is clear enough, Upgrade considers which commercial relationships may be relevant to the discussion.

03

Align the information

The parties need enough shared information to understand the product, specifications, documents and commercial context.

04

Coordinate the introduction

Where there is a meaningful basis for discussion, Upgrade coordinates the introduction and supports communication between the parties.

Our Role

Upgrade facilitates the commercial connection.

The buyer and seller remain responsible for their own decisions, negotiations, agreements and obligations.

The objective is a relevant conversation, not a long list of contacts.
